2. Money Outlook by Zodiac (One Line Each)

  • Aries: Set a spending cap before a quick purchase.
  • Taurus: Stabilize food and household spending without overbuying.
  • Gemini: Compare benefits briefly, then choose one useful offer.
  • Cancer: Give home and family spending a kind but clear limit.
  • Leo: Enjoy social spending while checking whether it repeats.
  • Virgo: Match the budget sheet to real account movement.
  • Libra: Check terms and cancellation dates before deposits.
  • Scorpio: Review debts, insurance, taxes, or shared funds once.
  • Sagittarius: Align expectations before splitting partner costs.
  • Capricorn: Judge work tools by real time saved.
  • Aquarius: Reuse existing tools before buying new gear.
  • Pisces: Choose rest, repairs, or a calmer room over mood shopping.

3. Weekly Action Checklist

1) Set one spending cap

Choose one category and write down a weekly cap you can apply immediately.

2) Record each payment immediately

3) Build a summer spending rule at the solstice

Rank travel, cooling, gathering, and vacation costs before summer spending grows.
