Zodiac Weekly Fortune Points (Mar 30–Apr 5)

Mar 30–Apr 5, 2026 | Cosmic Insight

Key takeaway: One-line guidance for all 12 signs plus 7 shared keywords for the coming week.

One-Line Fortune by 12 Signs

Aries - Fast starts are great, but add one midweek review to lock better outcomes.
Taurus - Your strength is consistency. Keep routine changes minimal this week.
Gemini - More messages and offers may appear; set your selection criteria first.
Cancer - Resolve relationship friction with short, clear conversations.
Leo - Visibility rises, but avoid overcommitting your schedule.
Virgo - Detail checks shine now, especially with documents and agreements.
Libra - Collaboration works best when roles are defined upfront.
Scorpio - Deep focus helps you clear one major delayed task.
Sagittarius - Travel and learning flow well; delay snap decisions by one day.
Capricorn - Practical reviews pay off. Recheck the numbers in long-term plans.
Aquarius - New ideas are strong, but test small before scaling.
Pisces - Set boundaries to reduce emotional drain; keep only two priorities.

7 Shared Keywords

1. Align - Reorder your top three priorities before acting.
2. Negotiate - Don’t accept the first terms without one revision.
3. Close - Finish one unfinished task this week, completely.
4. Simplify - Remove one item each from schedule, spending, and tasks.
5. Log - Keep short decision notes to avoid repeat mistakes.
6. Conservative - Start new moves small and verify response first.
7. Recover - Keep sleep timing consistent to hold your rhythm.

One-Line Action

Pick one item from your sign + one shared keyword, then execute both from Mar 30.

What to Avoid

Overbooking, snap conclusions, and overpromising. "Organize and simplify" works best this week.

Keywords are a compass, not a rulebook. Use them to align the week ahead, then move with ease.
